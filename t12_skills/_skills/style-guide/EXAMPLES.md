# Style Guide Examples

## Email Subject Lines

| Before (❌)                                   | After (✅)                          | Rule Applied                        |
|------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| URGENT: Action Required Regarding Your Account  | Let's fix your account access        | No urgency/caps, warm and direct     |
| Update                                          | Your invoice for March is ready      | Specific, not vague                  |
| We Have Processed Your Request Successfully     | Your request is done                 | Short, active voice, no jargon       |
| Notification: Password Reset Has Been Completed | You reset your password              | Active voice, under 50 characters    |

## Email Body Rewrites

**Before (❌):**
> Dear Valued Customer, We are pleased to inform you that your request has been processed successfully by our
> dedicated support team. Should you require any further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
> Sincerely, The Support Team

**After (✅):**
> Hi Alex,
>
> Good news — we've processed your request. You're all set.
>
> Need anything else? Just reply here and we'll take a look.
>
> Best,
> Jordan

**Changes**: removed passive voice ("has been processed" → "we've processed"), replaced formal opener/closer with a
direct greeting and first-name sign-off, cut filler phrases ("we are pleased to inform you", "should you require"),
added a clear next-step CTA.

## Social Media Rewrites

**LinkedIn — Before (❌):**
> We are excited to announce the successful completion of our latest product update, which incorporates a
> multitude of user-requested features and enhancements. #innovation #productupdate #excited

**LinkedIn — After (✅):**
> You asked, we built it. Our latest update adds the three features you've requested most — starting today.
>
> #productupdate #buildinpublic #customerfeedback

**Changes**: cut corporate phrasing ("we are excited to announce", "a multitude of"), opened with a direct hook,
trimmed hashtags to three relevant ones.

**Twitter/X — Before (❌):**
> We would like to take this opportunity to inform our followers that our service will be undergoing scheduled
> maintenance this weekend, which may result in temporary downtime.

**Twitter/X — After (✅):**
> Heads up: we're doing maintenance this weekend. Expect a few minutes of downtime. 🛠️

**Changes**: removed throat-clearing opener, shortened to fit the platform, added a light emoji for tone instead of
extra words.

## Support Reply Rewrites

**Before (❌):**
> We apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused. Unfortunately, this is a known issue and there is
> currently no resolution available at this time. We appreciate your patience and understanding.

**After (✅):**
> Sorry about that — that's frustrating. Here's what's going on: this is a known bug our team is actively fixing.
>
> In the meantime, try [workaround] — it should get you back up and running. We'll email you the moment it's
> resolved.

**Changes**: shifted from apology-only to empathy-first + solution-first, replaced vague reassurance ("we
appreciate your patience") with a concrete workaround and next step.
